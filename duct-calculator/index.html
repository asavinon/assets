A two-piece elbow or one offset fitting of angle A is made by cutting the pipe at A ÷ 2 off square, then rotating one piece 180° and rejoining. A rolling offset needs two fittings at the fitting angle — cut both the same.

Wrap-around layout

Wrap a strip of paper (or measure along the circumference with a flexible tape) around the pipe. Square a reference line around the pipe at the throat. At each mark distance around the pipe, measure up from the reference line by the rise in the table, connect the points with a smooth curve, and cut. Mark 0 is the throat (short side); the middle mark is the heel (long side). If you set a throat length, use the "up from pipe end" column instead — every mark is measured straight up from the squared end of the pipe, so the throat lands exactly where you want it.
All results are centerline dimensions — allow for seams, connectors, and crimp as your shop practice requires.
Formulas: true offset = √(rise² + roll²) · travel = offset ÷ sin(angle) · run = offset ÷ tan(angle) · roll rotation = atan(roll ÷ rise) · miter rise at θ = R·tan(cut)·(1 − cos θ)
