Inputs

A two-piece elbow or one offset fitting of angle A is made by cutting the pipe at A ÷ 2 off square, then rotating one piece 180° and rejoining. A rolling offset needs two fittings at the fitting angle — cut both the same.

Results

Wrap-around layout

Wrap a strip of paper (or measure along the circumference with a flexible tape) around the pipe. Square a reference line around the pipe at the throat. At each mark distance around the pipe, measure up from the reference line by the rise in the table, connect the points with a smooth curve, and cut. Mark 0 is the throat (short side); the middle mark is the heel (long side). If you set a throat length, use the "up from pipe end" column instead — every mark is measured straight up from the squared end of the pipe, so the throat lands exactly where you want it.

3D view — the cut piece

Drag to rotate · pinch or scroll to zoom. The orange face is the miter cut — throat on the low side, heel on the high side. Two of these joined with one piece spun 180° make the fitting.

Inputs

The throat radius is the inside curve of the fitting; the centerline radius runs down the middle of the pipe (CLR = throat radius + D ÷ 2). If the fitting has a straight collar or slip section past the curve, add its length — it extends the take-out.

Results

Diagram

Take-out is measured from the end of the fitting to the point where the two centerlines would intersect. Travel and run are figured centerline-to-centerline, so subtracting the take-out at each end gives the true cut length of the straight pipe between fittings: cut length = travel − 2 × take-out.

3D view — the fitting

Drag to rotate · pinch or scroll to zoom. The elbow built from your angle, diameter, and radius — green faces are the connection ends the take-out is measured from.
All results are centerline dimensions — allow for seams, connectors, and crimp as your shop practice requires.
Formulas: true offset = √(rise² + roll²) · travel = offset ÷ sin(angle) · run = offset ÷ tan(angle) · roll rotation = atan(roll ÷ rise) · miter rise at θ = R·tan(cut)·(1 − cos θ) · take-out = CLR·tan(angle ÷ 2) + collar
